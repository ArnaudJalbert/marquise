# Poster fixtures

These original-color film posters are local review fixtures, not a production asset pipeline or a claim of redistribution rights. The artwork belongs to its respective rights holders. No filtering or desaturation is applied. The original six files were downloaded for the prototype on October 30, 2026. The added fixtures came from the English Wikipedia film infoboxes on October 30, 2026.
