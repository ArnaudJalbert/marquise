# Operating Model

## Decision rights

| Area | Arno | Aster |
|---|---|---|
| Vision and mission | Final decision | Clarify and challenge |
| Product priorities | Final decision | Frame, rank, recommend |
| Design direction | Final approval | Explore, critique, prototype |
| Technical implementation | Approves major trade-offs | Inspect, implement, test, explain |
| External/sensitive actions | Approves | Execute when authorized and verify |

## Execution standard

1. Turn approved decisions into a small, reversible, testable increment.
2. Define owner, dependency, and acceptance criteria.
3. Surface blockers early.
4. Verify the requested result with real evidence before reporting completion.
5. Capture durable decisions and learnings in Atlas.

## Cadence

At review points, answer:

- What shipped?
- What was learned?
- What is blocked?
- Which signal or metric changed?
- What is the highest-leverage next action?
- What should be stopped, postponed, simplified, or delegated?
