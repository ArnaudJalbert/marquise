# Product Thesis

## Marquise

Marquise is a cinema showtimes aggregator for Montreal. Its core promise is to make relevant films and screenings easy to discover while keeping showtime information trustworthy.

## Strategic constraints

- Protect the Montreal web focus until evidence supports expansion.
- Treat data freshness, duplicate entities, stale showtimes, timezone errors, and broken links as product concerns.
- Prefer focused, reversible experiments over premature complexity.
- Optimize for user value, trust, retention, learning, and sustainable growth.

## Open assumptions

- A clearly designed discovery experience can reduce the effort of finding relevant screenings.
- Reliable, current showtime data is a meaningful differentiator.
